# BOA Consumer Complaint Analysis
Consumer complaints are a key indicator of customer dissatisfaction and operational inefficiencies. So, we are analyzing these complaints as they provide valuable insights into recurring issues, product-specific challenges, and areas where customer experience can be improved. 

### Tools
- Python: Used to improve data quality and accuracy by handling missing values, removing duplicate values, and correcting any discrepancies present in the data.
- Power BI: The advanced visualization provides insights related to data in the form of KPIs, charts, and graphs.

### Data Source
- From maven analytics( Consumer_Complaints.xlsx)

### Analysis
- 96.3% of complaints received a timely response, with the majority of them receiving an explanation from the bank regarding their issue.
- Significant fluctuations in complaint volumes during April-May and July-August for most of the years indicate potential seasonal trends or specific events driving consumer dissatisfaction.
- States with the highest complaints include California (13,258), Florida (6,227), Texas (4,509), and New York (4,300).
- From 2019-2022, the number of complaints is increasing rapidly, with most of them having complaints about checking or savings accounts and credit or prepaid cards.
- Major issues faced by consumers are managing an account, wrong purchases shown on bank statements, and incorrect information on the report.
- Most of the delayed responses are related to account management (including opening or closing accounts), credit statements, debt-related issues, and fraud or scam concerns.

### Strategy Recommendations
- Consumers should be provided with an online comprehensive guide about the possible issues they could face and how to resolve them. 
- The bank could reduce the systemic issues from their end by collaborating with credit reporting agencies.
- Allocate additional resources to high-complaint states (CA, FL, TX, NY) to address local consumer needs.
- Regularly review complaint data to identify emerging trends and adjust strategies accordingly.
- Improving customer verification processes to reduce frauds.
